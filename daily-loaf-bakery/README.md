The Daily Loaf is a fictional artisan bakery based in Cape Town, South Africa. This 5-page website allows customers to view menu items, place orders, and contact the bakery.

**Organisation Type:** Small Business (Retail Bakery)
**Target Audience:** Local residents, office workers, families
